## ❓ Common Questions About Hacking WiFi

**Hacker versus cracker — what's the distinction?**  
Hackers probe systems to strengthen defenses. Crackers exploit them for unauthorized entry. This repository targets the former: learning, auditing, and hardening.

**Can I legally crack my home router?**  
Absolutely — on equipment you purchased or on lab gear covered by a signed assessment letter. Targeting a stranger's SSID crosses into felony territory in most countries.

**What lets a cracker compromise weak WiFi?**  
Factory-default credentials, short dictionary words, absent WPA3, and unpatched vendor firmware. Running this toolkit against **your** AP reveals exactly those gaps.

**Will every network fall to this tool?**  
Unlikely. Random 16+ character passphrases, WPA3-only mode, and aggressive lockout policies stop casual attacks cold. Treat success metrics as motivation to upgrade — not as an invitation to trespass.
